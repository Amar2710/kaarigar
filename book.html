Privacy Policy
Aapka data 100% safe hai hamare paas
1. Hum kya collect karte hain?

Aapka naam, phone number, city aur skill — sirf tab jab aap register karte hain. Google se login karne pe aapki email aur naam milti hai. Location sirf aapki permission se access hoti hai.

2. Data ka use kaise hota hai?

Aapki information sirf booking aur profile dikhane ke liye use hoti hai. Hum aapka data kisi third party ko kabhi nahi bechte. Marketing mein bhi sirf aapki permission se use karte hain.

3. Security kaisi hai?

Firebase ka use hota hai jo Google ka enterprise-grade platform hai. Aapka data encrypted rehta hai — transit mein aur store hone ke baad bhi.

4. Aapke rights

Aap kabhi bhi apna account delete kar sakte hain. Aapki saari data turant remove ho jaati hai. Data export ke liye support@kaarigar.in pe email karo.

5. Cookies

Hum sirf zaruri cookies use karte hain — login session maintain karne ke liye. Koi tracking ya advertising cookies nahi.

6. Contact

Koi sawaal ho toh: privacy@kaarigar.in
